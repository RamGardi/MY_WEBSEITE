About the Game

Hollow Knight: Silksong is the epic sequel to Hollow Knight. Play as Hornet and journey through the mysterious kingdom of Pharloom. Explore new lands, discover powerful abilities, battle dangerous enemies and uncover ancient secrets.

Gameplay

Fast-Paced Combat : Hornet is much more nimble than the Knight from the first game, featuring a faster combat tempo, higher mobility, and the ability to speak.

Acrobatic Traversal : Moves include sprinting, a grappling hook, gliding, and complex midair combat maneuvers.

Silk Healing & Tools : Hornet heals using "silk" gathered by attacking enemies. You can also craft and equip color-coded tools (like Sting Shards) and unlockable crests that alter your move set and passive abilities.

World & Bosses: The interconnected world features over 200 distinct enemies and more than 40 boss encounters, accompanied by an orchestral score by Christopher Larkin.

Platforms : Available on PC (Windows, Linux, macOS), Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, PlayStation 4, PlayStation 5, Xbox One, and Xbox Series X/S.
