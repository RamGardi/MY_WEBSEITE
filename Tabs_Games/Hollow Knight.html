Hollow Knight

Hollow Knight is a 2D action-adventure game developed by Team Cherry. The game takes place in the mysterious underground kingdom of Hallownest, where players explore dangerous caves, fight powerful enemies and discover hidden secrets.

Action Adventure Indie 2D RPG Single Player Exploration

Game Information

Release Year: 2017

Developer: Team Cherry

Publisher: Team Cherry

Genre: Action, Adventure, Indie

Platform: PC, PlayStation, Xbox, Nintendo Switch

Game Mode: Single Player

About the Game

Hollow Knight takes players into Hallownest, a forgotten kingdom filled with strange creatures, mysterious locations and dangerous enemies. Players control a small knight who explores this underground world and uncovers its secrets.

Gameplay

The gameplay focuses on exploration, combat and platforming. Players can discover new abilities, defeat challenging bosses, collect items and unlock new areas throughout the adventure.

Key Features

• Beautiful hand-drawn 2D graphics

• Large interconnected world to explore

• Challenging enemies and boss fights

• Unique abilities and upgrades

• Atmospheric music and sound effects
