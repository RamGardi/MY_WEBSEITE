About the Game

Resident Evil 3 is a remake of the classic 1999 survival horror game. The story follows Jill Valentine, a former S.T.A.R.S. officer, as she tries to escape Raccoon City during a deadly zombie outbreak. She must survive dangerous creatures while being relentlessly hunted by Nemesis, a powerful bioweapon.

Gameplay

Resident Evil 3 features third-person survival horror gameplay. Players control Jill Valentine, explore dangerous locations, solve puzzles, collect weapons and supplies, and battle terrifying zombies. They must also escape from the relentless Nemesis.

Key Features

• Play as Jill Valentine and escape Raccoon City

• Survive the terrifying Nemesis

• Realistic graphics powered by the RE Engine

• Fast-paced third-person survival horror combat

• Explore dangerous locations and collect supplies
