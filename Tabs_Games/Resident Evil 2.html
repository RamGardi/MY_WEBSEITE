Resident Evil 2

About the Game

Resident Evil 2 is a remake of the classic 1998 survival horror game. Set in Raccoon City, the story follows Leon S. Kennedy and Claire Redfield as they try to survive a terrifying zombie outbreak. Players explore the police station, solve puzzles, collect resources and uncover the truth behind the disaster.

Gameplay

Resident Evil 2 features third-person survival horror gameplay. Players explore dangerous locations, solve puzzles, manage limited ammunition and healing supplies, and fight terrifying zombies and powerful enemies.
