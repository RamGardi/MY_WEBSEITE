About the Game

Resident Evil 4 Remake is a modern reimagining of the classic 2005 survival horror game. Players follow Leon S. Kennedy, who travels to a remote village in Europe to rescue Ashley Graham, the kidnapped daughter of the U.S. President. As Leon investigates, he discovers a mysterious cult and terrifying infected villagers.

Gameplay

Resident Evil 4 Remake features third-person action and survival horror gameplay. Players control Leon S. Kennedy, explore dangerous villages and castles, fight infected enemies, solve puzzles and manage weapons and resources. The game combines intense combat with exploration and suspense.

Key Features

• Play as Leon S. Kennedy on a rescue mission

• Explore mysterious villages, castles and islands

• Fight infected enemies and powerful bosses

• Modern graphics powered by the RE Engine

• Upgrade weapons and manage survival resources

• Experience third-person action and horror
